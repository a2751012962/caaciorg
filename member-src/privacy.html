Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

The Chinese American Association of Central Illinois (“CAACI,” “we,” “us”) is a community non-profit organization. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf, and how you can reach us about it.

Information we collect

We collect only what the site needs in order to work:

  • Account: your email address and, if you register with a password, a securely hashed version of that password. We never store your password itself.
  • Membership profile: your name, phone number, membership tier, membership status and expiry date, and any family members you are grouped with in a household membership.
  • Payments: the amount, date, membership tier and any discount code for each membership payment or donation, together with the reference numbers Stripe assigns to the transaction. Donations may include a donor name.
  • Forms you submit: the name, email, phone number and message you enter in the contact form, and the business details you enter when submitting a business-directory listing.
  • Events: which events you RSVP to and how many guests you bring.

We do not collect your payment card details. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Signing in with Google or Microsoft

If you choose to sign in with Google or Microsoft, that provider tells us your email address and your name so we can create and identify your membership account. We do not receive your password, and we do not request access to your mail, files, contacts or calendar. You can instead register with an email address and a password of your own.

Payments

Membership fees and donations are processed by Stripe. Your card number is entered on Stripe’s own payment page and is never sent to or stored on our servers — we keep only the transaction reference, amount and date so that staff can confirm your membership and issue refunds. Stripe handles your card data under its own privacy policy.

Your digital membership card

Active members can display a membership card carrying a QR code. Scanning that code opens a public verification page that shows only your name, your membership tier and whether the membership is currently valid. It does not reveal your email address, phone number, payment history or any other detail.

Who else processes your information

We rely on a small number of service providers. They process your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — stores the membership database, account credentials and uploaded images.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and delivers this website.
  • Stripe — processes payments and refunds.
  • Resend — delivers the emails we send you, such as confirmations and announcements.
  • Google and Microsoft — only if you choose to sign in with one of them.

Beyond these providers, we disclose personal information only when the law requires it.

Who can see your information

Signed-in members can see their own profile and their own payment history. Authorized CAACI staff can see member records in order to run the organization — to confirm memberships, answer questions, issue refunds and send announcements. Business-directory listings and event information are public once approved or published.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember your language choice. These are necessary for the site to function; we do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep membership and payment records for as long as you are a member and afterwards for as long as we need them for the organization’s accounting and reporting obligations. Contact-form messages are kept while we deal with your enquiry. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

You can view and correct your own details on your account page at any time. Write to us at the address below to request a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or close your account. Deleting your account removes your login and membership record; we may keep the minimum payment records our accounting obligations require.

Children

This website is intended for adults. Children are named on a family membership only by the adult who manages that household membership; children do not create their own accounts here.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
